PHONICS TUBE · FOR THE GROWN-UPS

Ready for your next
little breakthrough?

A simple routine for a classroom, a small group, or a few minutes together at home.

  1. Find the lesson your child knows.

    Enter the UFLI lesson number. Follow your teacher’s sequence.

  2. Preview one short video.

    Read the teaching note, check the pronunciation, and watch it yourself first. “Start here” is a suggested choice, not a teacher approval.

  3. Watch. Say. Read. Write.

    After watching, say the sound, blend three familiar words, then write one. Keep it short and encouraging.

  4. Share the little win.

    Copy a lesson link for a colleague or family. Bookmark a favourite for next time.

Before you press play

YouTube may show ads and may not work in mainland China. Built-in practice uses no YouTube. Review notes and feedback stay on your device; export them to share with your team.

FOR FAMILIES & TEACHERS
✦

A little help for
your little reader.

Phonics Tube brings short videos and word practice together in the order of the UFLI Foundations scope and sequence. It is an independent resource, not an official UFLI product.

Start with the lesson your child knows

Videos reinforce a skill; they don’t replace a teacher’s lesson. Some cover several patterns or include words beyond the current lesson. Check each resource’s alignment note and preview it together.

Different voices, familiar sounds

Some clips use American English and others use British or Australian English. Check the pronunciation note and model the sound used in your classroom. A video may support only part of a lesson.

Watching from mainland China?

Choose Practice only at the top. Our built-in word cards use no YouTube, external fonts, or remote images. YouTube videos may be unavailable without a VPN. We haven’t rehosted any creator’s videos.

A library that respects creators

Video links and listed runtimes were checked during research; full instructional review is still pending. Original files can be added after permission is recorded. China playback has not yet been tested on mainland networks.

Private by design

No accounts, ads from us, or child profiles. Saved resources and history stay in this browser. Opening a YouTube player connects to YouTube, whose own policies apply.
